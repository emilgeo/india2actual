## What it fixes

Your bank describes two orders from the same shop with a different reference
number each time, so Actual sees two payees:

| What your bank writes                                  | What Actual gets |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------- |
| `UPI/DR/412345678901/SWIGGY/YESB/swiggy@ybl/Payment`   | Swiggy           |
| `UPI/DR/419876543210/SWIGGY/YESB/swiggy@ybl/Payment`   | Swiggy           |

The bank's original text is kept in Notes, so nothing is lost.

## Which banks work

Tested on real statements: ICICI (savings and credit card, PDF), Federal Bank
(savings, PDF), IDFC FIRST Bank (Excel) and CSB Bank (CSV). Other banks are
expected to work but are not yet confirmed. See the full list in the
[docs](/docs/#supported-banks), and if you try one, a report of what worked or
failed is very welcome.

## Why this exists

Actual has no bank sync for Indian banks, and is unlikely to get one soon.
Reading bank data in India goes through the Account Aggregator framework, which
is open to registered financial institutions with a commercial contract, not to
a free open-source app. So the route is to download a statement and import it.
india2actual makes that painless.
